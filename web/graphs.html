1. The procedure graph — what order things must happen in

This is the bank's refund procedure, drawn as a graph. Boxes are steps. Arrows are the only moves that are allowed. Click any box to see what has to be true before that step may run.

In one sentence: every other control in Aegis asks "may this agent use this tool?" — this graph asks "is this the right moment?". A refund with no decision behind it is a perfectly valid tool call at completely the wrong time, and only this catches it.

2. The knowledge graph — who is related to whom

Every fact the bank knows, as dots and lines. Customers, their email addresses, their tickets, the tools, the agents. Click any dot to see what it connects to.

Why it exists: a rule can check an amount or a capability on its own. It cannot check a relationship — "is this email address actually this customer's?" Two real bugs in this project were exactly that question going unasked.
